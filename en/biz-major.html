    Major Projects

    Representative projects carried out by DK ENGINEERING.

    • 01

      Kori Unit 1 Main Control Board and Auxiliary Equipment Replacement

      • Replaced the Kori Unit 1 main control board and auxiliary equipment with environmentally and seismically qualified equipment (safety system control panels, reactor control panels, turbine control panels, alarm system upgrade, etc.)
    • 02

      MCC Breaker Panel Replacement for Kori Units 3·4 and Hanbit Units 1·2

      • Full replacement of MCC breakers due to aging and discontinued parts
      • Compartments replaced in sequence at every overhaul (OH)
    • 03

      Control Element Drive Mechanism Control System (CEDMCS) Installation

      • Removed the aging control rod control system and replaced it with the latest digital system built on redundant controllers
      • Shin-Kori Units 1·2 / Shin-Wolsong Units 1·2 / Doosan Heavy Industries (client)
    • 04

      TBM (Tunnel Boring Machine) Design and Manufacturing Localization

      • Development of core design and manufacturing technology for full-face tunnel boring TBMs
      • Localized design and manufacturing of electrical equipment and circuits
      • Lead research institute: EM Korea Co., Ltd. / Joint research institute: Daekyeong Engineering Co., Ltd.
    • 05

      Hanul Units 1·2 Main Generator and Excitation System Upgrade

      • New replacement of the main generator and excitation system at the end of design life and due to discontinued parts
      • Doosan Heavy Industries (client) / design, installation, construction and technical support
    • 06

      KNPC CFP MAA FAR E-HOUSE E&I PROJECT

      • Electrical and instrumentation installation of a containerized power distribution system housing all equipment for power distribution and control supply in a container
    • 07

      Hyundai Oilbank 3000 PPH CCR MODULE E&I PROJECT

      • CCR (Continuous Catalyst Regeneration) process
      • Electrical and instrumentation construction and installation for UOP LLC / GS Entec (client)
    • 08

      Energy Technology Development through National R&D Projects

      • Laser-based decontamination system for reducing solid radioactive waste
      • Gas-hydrate-based mobile modular liquid waste treatment system for nuclear decommissioning
      • Wear-resistant surface treatment materials and processes for nuclear valve seats and discs
      • Blockchain-based IoT platform for radioactive waste management
