    Services

    A leading company in nuclear, thermal power and plant electrical & instrumentation

    Building on outstanding nuclear expertise, many qualifications and plant know-how, Daekyeong Engineering is recognized as a company that can deliver both design services and construction for major nuclear projects such as plant computer systems, main generators and main power systems, and main control board replacements, as well as for nuclear power facilities and research reactors. We are also expanding into thermal, pumped-storage and new & renewable hybrid energy projects.

    Holding safety-class qualifications for nuclear electrical design and electrical panel maintenance, Daekyeong Engineering provides customers with the best service through high-quality construction and engineering, continuous technology development and strong competitiveness.

    As lead organization of many national and nuclear decommissioning R&D projects, we are also accelerating the development of new technologies and products.
