    SAFETY & HEALTH

    Health & Safety Management

    Safety at power plant and plant sites and the health of our people are the foundation of our management, governed by a KS Q ISO 45001 system.

    • 01HSE Team

      The HSE team, reporting directly to the CEO, handles site safety, health and environmental matters.

    • 02Maintaining ISO 45001

      First certified for occupational health and safety management in 2015, we have maintained certification through renewal audits.

    • 03Contribution to Safe Nuclear Operation

      Received plaques of appreciation from KHNP for contribution to safe operation of Uljin Units 1 & 2 (2009) and Kori Unit 1 (2017).
