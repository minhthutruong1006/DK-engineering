    CEO Message

    We will do our best to satisfy our customers with the finest technology.

    CEO, Daekyeong Engineering Co., Ltd. Young-Ha Lim

    With the finest technology, we strive for
    customer satisfaction.

    Aiming to become a global leader in the power industry, all employees of Daekyeong Engineering are united under our motto, "Technology first, competitiveness secured," to grow the company. With technology and know-how built over many years, Daekyeong Engineering is the only small and medium-sized company in Korea able to handle the entire process at once, from early-stage design to manufacturing, installation and commissioning. Based on diverse project experience, we are becoming a leader in new technology and nuclear decommissioning, working across nuclear, thermal, pumped-storage and new & renewable hybrid energy facilities, petrochemical, shipbuilding and offshore plants and other industrial plants, and taking part in many major national R&D projects.

    The 21st-century power industry's
    Global Leader Daekyeong Engineering Co., Ltd.

    Going forward, Daekyeong Engineering will contribute to the development of the power industry and keep growing as its global leader through high-quality engineering based on design specialization, complete manufacturing and construction backed by systematic quality activities, and bold investment in technology development and talent.

    We sincerely thank everyone who has supported Daekyeong Engineering, and we will do our best to be a "trusted company" with you anytime, anywhere.

    Young-Ha Lim, CEO, Daekyeong Engineering Co., Ltd.
