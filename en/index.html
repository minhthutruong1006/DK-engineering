    Merit

    Technology first, competitiveness secured

    The company motto Daekyeong Engineering has kept for 20 years.

    • Merit 01
      From design to commissioning, all in oneDesign · Manufacturing · Installation · Commissioning
      The only small and medium-sized company in Korea able to handle the whole process at once, from early-stage design to manufacturing, installation and commissioning.
    • Merit 02
      Nuclear qualifications
      We hold KHNP "Q" class for operating-plant electrical design and for electrical cable and panel maintenance, "A" class for lighting equipment maintenance, and maintenance qualification from the five power generation companies.

      201 design & engineering projects and 384 nuclear & plant construction projects, from the Kori, Hanbit, Hanul and Wolsong nuclear plants to overseas projects in Kuwait, Thailand, Taiwan and the USA.

      Leading in nuclear decommissioning
      With 19 national R&D projects and 10 patents, we have developed laser decontamination and radioactive waste management technology. We received the Busan Mayor's commendation for decommissioning in 2023 and the Presidential Commendation in 2026.
